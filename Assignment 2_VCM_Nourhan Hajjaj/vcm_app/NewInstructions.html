Please read the new instructions

You are now randomly assigned to a new group of {{ C.PLAYERS_PER_GROUP }} participants, including yourself.
You will be rematched every round with new players for the next 5 rounds.

At the beginning of each round, you receive {{ C.ENDOWMENT }}. You choose how much of your endowment you would like to contribute to the group project.

Each point contributed to the group project, it will be saved in a group account . The total group contributions inside this group account is then multiplied by {{ C.MPCR_2 }} and each individual share is distributed among the group members equally, regardless of how much they personally contributed.

Round earnings

As before, for each round, after everyone submit their contribution.
You will see the total contributions by the group , and your individual share for each contribution

Your round results are now calculated accordingly:
Your initial endowment − your contribution + (total group contribution × {{ C.MPCR_2}})

Final Payoffs

Every round counts toward your final payment.
Your final payment is the summation of total endowment left from each round + individual share from the public account
