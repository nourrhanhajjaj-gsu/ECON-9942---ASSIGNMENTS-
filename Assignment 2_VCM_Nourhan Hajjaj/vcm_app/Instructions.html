The Group Project

You are randomly assigned to a group of {{ C.PLAYERS_PER_GROUP }} participants, including yourself.

You will stay with the same group for the next 5 rounds, and then you will be randomly assigned to different groups starting of round 6.
You will play for a total of {{C.NUM_ROUNDS}} rounds.

At the beginning of each round, you receive {{ C.ENDOWMENT }}. You choose how much of your endowment you would like to contribute to the group project.

Each point contributed to the group project, it will be saved in a group account.

You receive a new endowment of $10 each round. You can only contribute from the endowment, not your accumulated earnings.
The total group contributions inside this group account is then multiplied by {{ C.MPCR_1 }} and each individual share is distributed among the group members equally, regardless of how much they personally contributed.

Round Earnings

For each round, after everyone submit their contribution.
You will see the total contributions by the group , and your individual share for each contribution.

Your round results are calculated accordingly:
Your initial endowment − your contribution + (total group contribution × {{ C.MPCR_1 }})

Final Payoff

Every round counts toward your final payment.
Your final payment is the summation of total endowment left from each round + individual share from the public account
